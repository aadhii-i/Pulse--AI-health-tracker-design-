Core Modules

Everything health, in one place

Four carefully designed modules cover every dimension of personal health — from biometrics to mental wellness.

🔐
Authentication & Onboarding
A beautifully crafted first-run experience with animated onboarding, secure auth flows, and guided profile setup.
  • Animated splash & 4-screen onboarding with SVG illustrations
  • Login, Signup, OTP Verification & Password reset
  • 4-step profile setup: info, goals, medical, permissions
  • Social auth (Google & Apple) with privacy-first design
  • Password strength indicator & accessible form validation
❤️
Health Metrics
Real-time tracking of 8+ vital metrics with live charts, trend analysis, and AI-powered health insights.
  • Heart rate with live ECG waveform & zone breakdown
  • Blood pressure gauge with systolic/diastolic trends
  • Blood oxygen SpO₂ ring meter with 24h monitoring
  • Glucose CGM-style graphs with meal correlation
  • Weight/BMI tracking with 6-month trend & body composition
🏃
Workouts & Wellness
Comprehensive fitness and mental health tracking — from live GPS running sessions to guided meditation with biofeedback.
  • Workout dashboard with 6 activity types & weekly calendar
  • Live GPS running tracker with pace, HR zone & map overlay
  • Meditation player with breathing animation & HRV biofeedback
  • Stress monitor with semicircle gauge & contributing factors
  • Mood journal with emoji check-in, tags & 7-day history chart
🩺
Care & Reports
Complete healthcare management with medication reminders, doctor appointments, monthly reports and one-tap emergency SOS.
  • Emergency SOS with ripple animation & emergency contacts
  • Medication schedule with adherence tracking & refill alerts
  • Doctor appointment planner with pre-visit checklist
  • Monthly AI health report with correlations & recommendations
  • Settings, Premium subscription & notification centre
